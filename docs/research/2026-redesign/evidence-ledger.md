## Search and follow-up sequence
1. Scope: UK online whole-household shop, parents and low digital confidence; research navigation, cognitive load, repeat use, colour and relevant competitor flows. Separate currently working planning from future live retailer integration.
2. Discovery batches: official Apple/Android navigation; W3C target/motion; NN/g onboarding; parent household-load research; Mealime/Cozi; Samsung Food/Sidekick/Bring; parent-facing Huckleberry/Peanut.
3. Targeted follow-ups: original Wiley paper and exact sample; Tuch original publication; colour review abstract; Duolingo exact experiment and relative outcome; Google HEART original publication; Samsung planner instructions; Mealime official closure notice.
4. Direct visual inspection: successful public-source retrieval followed by browser review of Mealime, Huckleberry and Peanut. No account creation, checkout or submission. Features not directly interacted with are attributed to product documentation.
5. Disconfirming checks: colour is context-dependent; screenshots measure aesthetics, not retention; product ratings/marketing figures cannot establish causality; Mealime is closing; daily learning streaks do not map directly to weekly shopping. PMC11761833 hit a verification gate and is not cited as a full-text review.
6. Stop: material decisions had direct support with explicit limits. Further broad searches repeated patterns; remaining gaps require user studies or genuine retailer access, not more ranked app lists.
